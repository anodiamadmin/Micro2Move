Now micro2Move project is changing form peer-to-peer e-bike hire to e-bike businesses like PEDL or 99Bikes franchisees from Sydney listing or renting out e-bikes on the platform. I shall ask subsequent questions regarding designing the platform:
Through the Micro2Move Business website, e-bike shops and businesses, hence forth called organizational suppliers or OS-es, will log in and list a series of e-bikes and put in how many of those e-bikes are available. later on they can view/ update the number. They can also view how many of them are booked or rented on a particular date or date range.
